Privacy Policy — MQS ChatPilot

Last updated: 2026-08-30 • MQS TECH, Calatagan, Batangas

MQS ChatPilot is a Messenger auto-reply booking assistant for balsa owners in Calatagan. We value your privacy.

What we collect

  • Messages you send to the Page (to generate replies)
  • Booking info you provide (date, pax, name, contact) — to create inquiries
  • GCash screenshot (Ref/Amount only, via AI vision) — to verify payment
  • Account info (email, balsa name) when you register on mqschatpilot.com

How we use it

To auto-reply on Messenger, check availability, create bookings, verify GCash, send reminders/reviews (with Meta-approved tags), and notify the balsa owner.

Sharing

We do not sell data. Only the balsa owner (Page admin) and our server (Render/Supabase) can see bookings. Facebook and Gemini APIs are used to send/receive messages and generate replies.

Storage

Bookings and accounts are stored in Supabase (encrypted at rest) and retained until you request deletion. Message history (sessions.json) is kept 12 messages for context, then truncated.

Your rights

You can request deletion via MQS TECH Facebook (https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61553767379827) or Telegram https://t.me/Nicanicapotz. We will delete your data within 7 days.

Contact

MQS TECH — Calatagan, Batangas — support via Facebook Messenger or Telegram.
