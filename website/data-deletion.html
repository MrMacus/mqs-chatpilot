Data Deletion Instructions — MQS ChatPilot

How to request deletion of your data

If you want us to delete your data collected via Messenger (messages, bookings) or via mqschatpilot.com (account, business info):

  1. Message us on Facebook: Go to MQS ChatPilot on Facebook and send “Delete my data — [your email or Page name]”.
  2. Or Telegram: Message @Nicanicapotz with same request.
  3. Or Email: Send to emdee.bernal@gmail.com subject “Delete My Data” with your email/Page ID.

We will delete your Messenger history, bookings, and account data from Supabase within 7 days and confirm via the same channel.

For Facebook’s own data, you can also use Facebook’s account deletion flow.
